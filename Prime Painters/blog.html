Informed Decision

DIY vs. Professional Painting

Weighing whether to tackle your next paint project yourself or hire an expert? Compare the time, cost, equipment, and outcome factors to decide the best path forward.

Hands-On Approach

DIY Painting

Best for smaller weekend refreshes and personal creative projects.

  • ✓ Lower upfront labour cost: Save on professional service fees by investing your own sweat equity.
  • ✕ Requires significant time: Demands full weekends of prep, painting, and cleanup.
  • ✕ You handle preparation: Patching, sanding, and taping fall entirely on your shoulders.
  • ✕ Equipment purchases: Ladders, drop cloths, rollers, and trays must be bought separately.
  • ! Experience-dependent results: Finish quality relies entirely on your personal skill level.

When DIY makes sense:

Small accent walls, single bedrooms, minor touch-ups, or when you genuinely enjoy hands-on crafting projects.

Recommended for Best Results
Expert Craftsmanship

Professional Painting

Best for whole-home projects, exteriors, cabinets, and flawless finishes.

  • ✓ Professional workmanship: Flawless cutting, sharp lines, and uniform coating thickness.
  • ✓ Saves valuable time: Experienced crews complete multi-room projects in days instead of weeks.
  • ✓ Professional preparation: Advanced patching, sanding, priming, and defect correction included.
  • ✓ Contractor supplies equipment: Commercial sprayers, scaffolding, and professional tools provided.
  • ✓ Experienced application: Specialized techniques for high-wear areas, exteriors, and cabinetry.

When hiring a professional is better:

Exterior facades, multi-story ceilings, kitchen cabinet spraying, extensive wall repairs, or when you want guaranteed results without the hassle.

Expert Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Get clear, detailed insights into painting costs, timelines, preparation standards, and contractor procedures.

How much does it cost to paint a house? +

Painting costs vary depending on the total square footage, whether the project is interior or exterior, the height of the ceilings, and the condition of the surfaces. Additional factors include high-end trim work, cabinet spraying, and extensive surface prep like repairing cracks or moisture damage. Getting multiple quotes from vetted contractors is the best way to determine precise pricing for your specific project.

How long does painting take? +

A single standard room can typically be prepped and painted by professionals in 1 to 2 days. A full interior home painting project usually takes between 3 to 7 days, depending on the number of rooms, detail work, and drying times between coats. Exterior painting timelines depend heavily on weather conditions and drying times for primers and sealants.

Should I move furniture before painting? +

Yes, homeowners are typically asked to remove small items, electronics, and fragile valuables from the room prior to the crew's arrival. Large furniture pieces can usually be shifted to the center of the room and safely covered with heavy-duty drop cloths by the painting team to protect them from dust and paint splatters.

How many coats of paint are needed? +

Most professional paint jobs require two coats of finish paint over a properly applied primer. Two coats ensure true color richness, uniform sheen, and proper durability. If you are painting a dark wall a significantly lighter shade (or vice versa), an extra coat or tinted primer may be required for full coverage.

Do painters repair cracks? +

Professional painters include standard surface preparation—such as filling hairline cracks, patching nail holes, and sanding rough spots—as part of their service. However, major structural cracks, severe water damage, or extensive drywall replacement should be discussed and outlined in the quote beforehand.

Can you paint over old paint? +

Yes, provided the existing paint is in good condition, clean, and securely adhered to the wall. The surface must be washed and lightly sanded to create a mechanical bond. If the old paint is peeling, glossy, or oil-based, a specialized primer is required before applying new topcoats.

How often should a house be repainted? +

Interior walls generally need repainting every 5 to 7 years, though high-traffic areas like hallways, kitchens, and children's rooms may need touch-ups sooner. Exterior paint typically lasts between 5 to 10 years depending on local climate exposure, weather extremes, and the quality of the original coating.

What is the best paint finish for walls? +

Matte or eggshell finishes are ideal for living rooms and bedrooms because they absorb light and hide surface imperfections. For high-moisture or high-traffic areas like kitchens, bathrooms, and corridors, satin or semi-gloss finishes are recommended due to their superior scrubbability and moisture resistance.

Does exterior painting include preparation? +

Professional exterior painting always includes essential preparation steps such as high-pressure washing to remove dirt and mildew, scraping loose or peeling paint, caulking gaps, and applying exterior-grade primers. Thorough preparation is critical to ensure the new paint binds correctly and withstands weather elements.

Can I get multiple painting quotes? +

Yes, comparing multiple quotes is highly recommended. It allows you to evaluate different pricing structures, verify what prep work and paint brands are included, and ensure you find a reliable contractor who fits your project budget and timeline requirements.
