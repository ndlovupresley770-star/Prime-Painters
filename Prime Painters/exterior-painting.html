Cracks & Imperfections

The Problem

Minor cracks and surface imperfections compromise structural integrity and appearance.

Preparation

Thorough filling, patching, and smoothing are applied before any coating begins.

✓ Result: Seamless, uniform wall canvases.

Peeling & Flaking Paint

The Problem

Aged, brittle coatings lose adherence, leading to bubbling, blistering, and flaking.

Preparation

Old loose coatings are scraped, sanded, and smoothed to bare stable substrates.

✓ Result: Maximum bond for fresh topcoats.

Chalking Surfaces

The Problem

Older exterior paint breaks down under UV exposure, leaving a powdery residue.

Preparation

High-pressure washing and appropriate prep treatments clear away powdery residue.

✓ Result: A clean, receptive surface texture.

Damp & Moisture

The Problem

Trapped moisture leads to bubbling and paint failure. Paint does not fix structural damp.

Preparation

Underlying moisture sources are assessed and resolved before coating work proceeds.

✓ Result: Long-term protection against water damage.

Mould & Algae

The Problem

Biological growth accumulates rapidly on shaded, damp exterior zones.

Preparation

Professionals treat and wash affected surfaces thoroughly prior to painting.

✓ Result: Sanitized, contamination-free walls.

Protective Systems

Exterior Paint & Coating Options

Selecting the right coating is critical for long-term durability. Explore our specialized exterior product categories tailored for specific surfaces and environmental challenges.

01

Acrylic Exterior Paint

The industry standard for residential exterior walls, premium acrylic formulations provide outstanding resistance against harsh weather exposure, UV rays, and moisture penetration while maintaining superior colour retention over years of sunlight.

  • ✓ Exceptional weather and moisture resistance
  • ✓ High flexibility to handle thermal expansion
  • ✓ Long-lasting colour vibrancy and durability
Best for: General residential siding, stucco, and brick walls.
02

Elastomeric Coatings

High-build, rubberized formulas designed specifically for masonry and rendered surfaces where exceptional elasticity and hairline crack-bridging properties are needed. Note that while they bridge non-structural hairline cracking, they do not eliminate underlying structural damage.

  • ✓ Advanced crack-bridging flexibility
  • ✓ Heavy-duty moisture barrier protection
  • ✓ Thick film build for superior coverage
Best for: Rendered walls, concrete structures, and cement plaster.
03

Masonry Coatings

Engineered specifically to bond with mineral substrates, these breathable yet protective coatings safeguard stone, brick, and concrete against alkali attack and moisture ingress while enhancing the building’s overall aesthetic appeal.

  • ✓ Vapor-permeable structure preventing blistering
  • ✓ Alkali and efflorescence resistance
  • ✓ Clean, uniform architectural finish
Best for: Plaster, concrete, brickwork, and rendered feature walls.
04

Roof Coatings

Formulated to endure extreme UV exposure and continuous weather stress. These protective coatings shield tiles and metal roofs while dramatically upgrading visual curb appeal. Note: Coatings provide protective weathering seals but do not replace structural roof repairs or fix active leaks alone.

  • ✓ Maximum solar UV reflection & defense
  • ✓ Heavy-duty waterproofing surface protection
  • ✓ Revitalized color and clean aesthetics
Best for: Suitable concrete tiles, corrugated iron, and sheet roofs.
05

Metal Coatings

Specialized rust-inhibiting systems designed to withstand corrosion on steelwork and iron. Long-term performance relies on a strict three-step protocol: Preparation (derusting/cleaning), Priming (anti-corrosive base), and Protective Finish.

  • ✓ Inhibits rust and oxidation spread
  • ✓ Industrial-grade adhesion primers
  • ✓ Tough, impact-resistant topcoats
Best for: Gates, security railings, metal doors, and exterior steelwork.
06

Wood Stains & Finishes

Penetrating oils, transparent stains, and opaque finishes designed to nourish timber and prevent warping, cracking, or UV greying. The correct product selection strictly depends on the timber species and the desired natural or rich visual appearance.

  • ✓ Deep nourishing timber hydration
  • ✓ UV inhibitors to stop wood greying
  • ✓ Moisture-repellent barrier protection
Best for: Timber doors, pergolas, fascias, and outdoor wood structures.

Investment Insight

Exterior Painting Cost Guide

Understand the key factors that influence the cost of your exterior painting project. We believe in transparency to help you plan your investment effectively.

Property Size

The total surface area is the primary driver. Larger homes or multi-story buildings require substantially more paint volume, specialized access equipment, and labour hours to complete.

Surface Condition

Extensive preparation work is often the most time-consuming element. Heavily peeling paint, deep cracks, surface contamination, and neglected substrates demand significant labour before coating begins.

Surface Type & Access

Different materials (stucco, weatherboard, metal, roof tiles) require distinct primers and application techniques. Furthermore, difficult-to-access areas or multi-story properties requiring scaffolding increase complexity and cost.

Paint Quality & System

Premium, high-build exterior paints offer superior durability, UV protection, and coverage but come at a higher upfront material cost compared to standard budget options. The specified number of coats also impacts total material usage.

Necessary Repairs

Any structural or substrate repairs identified during assessment—such as plaster mending, crack stitching, fascia replacement, or damp-proofing preparation—are essential for a lasting finish but are separate from pure painting labour.

Detail & Finish Level

The complexity of the project, including the number of trim colours, detailed feature work on windows/pergolas, and the overall standard of finish required, will influence the precision labour time.

Got Questions?

Exterior Painting FAQ

Find clear, professional answers to the most common questions about exterior property painting and maintenance.

Q. How often should a house exterior be painted?

The ideal timeframe depends heavily on the coating quality, surface material, local climate conditions, UV exposure, and the condition of the existing paint. High-quality exterior coatings generally protect your home for anywhere from 5 to 10 years.

Q. How long does exterior painting take?

Project duration varies based on the overall property size, the extent of surface preparation required, weather conditions, and specific multi-coat system specifications. Most residential exterior projects take anywhere from a few days to a couple of weeks.

Q. Does exterior painting include pressure washing?

Yes, thorough cleaning and pressure washing form an essential part of the preparation process where appropriate. Removing dust, dirt, and biological growth ensures maximum adhesion for new primers and topcoats.

Q. Can you paint over peeling paint?

Loose or failing paint generally needs appropriate mechanical preparation first. Scraping, sanding, and priming compromised areas are vital steps to prevent the new paint layer from failing alongside the old coating.

Q. Do painters repair cracks before painting?

Minor surface repairs may be included depending on the agreed project scope. However, significant structural problems, deep subsidence cracks, or major damp issues require specialized professional assessment and remediation before painting.

Q. Can you paint boundary walls and metal surfaces?

Yes, boundary walls and exterior metalwork (such as gates, fences, and railings) are very common exterior projects. They require specialized masonry or anti-corrosive metal coating systems to ensure long-term durability.

Q. Can exterior painting help protect walls from weather?

Appropriate exterior coatings provide a robust protective weather barrier against UV rays, rain, and thermal expansion. Keep in mind, however, that paint is not a structural fix for underlying moisture or masonry defects.

Q. Can you paint during rainy weather?

Moisture and rain severely compromise exterior coating performance. Contractors must monitor forecasts and wait for dry, suitable ambient conditions before applying primers or topcoats.

Q. Do I need to be home while the painters work?

This depends on the property layout, site access arrangements, and your contractor. For exterior projects, homeowners often do not need to stay home as long as painters have clear outdoor access to water, electricity, and work areas.
