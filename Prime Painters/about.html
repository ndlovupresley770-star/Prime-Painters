How We Vet Our Painting Professionals

Not Every Painter Makes the Cut

Our rigorous 5-step screening process ensures you receive unmatched craftsmanship, safety, and accountability on every project.

Absolute Trust Through Rigorous Screening

We don't leave your property's finish to chance. Every contractor on our platform undergoes strict multi-point verification before they ever pick up a brush at your home.

STEP 1

Business & Identity Verification

We verify official company registration, directorship records, and legal identification for complete operational transparency.

STEP 2

Insurance Verification

Active public liability and damage insurance coverage is verified to protect your property against unexpected accidents.

STEP 3

Experience & Workmanship Review

We inspect past portfolios, surface prep methodologies, and technical application standards across residential and commercial jobs.

STEP 4

References & Reputation Checks

Direct client feedback and historical project track records are analyzed to ensure reliable scheduling and polite crew conduct.

STEP 5

Ongoing Quality Monitoring

Continuous homeowner performance reviews and satisfaction scores maintain high accountability across active network rosters.
