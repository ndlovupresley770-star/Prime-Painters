Kitchens and bathrooms endure heavy daily usage, humidity, and temperature shifts. Our specialized topcoats resist chipping, scratching, and moisture degradation to maintain their beauty for years.

Step-by-Step Methodology

Our Cabinet Refinishing Process

Refinishing is far more than a simple coat of paint. Discover our meticulous 8-step craftsmanship designed for factory-smooth durability.

01

Assessment & Colour Consultation

The contractor assesses the existing cabinet material, condition, hardware and desired finish.

02

Removal & Labelling

Cabinet doors and drawer fronts are carefully removed and labelled so everything can be returned to its correct position.

03

Deep Cleaning & Degreasing

Grease, cooking residue, dirt and contaminants are thoroughly removed prior to surface prep.

04

Sanding & Surface Preparation

Surfaces are meticulously prepared to improve adhesion and create a completely smooth foundation.

05

Professional Priming

A suitable high-adhesion primer is applied according to the specific cabinet material and coating system.

06

Spray Application

Professional spraying equipment is used to achieve an even, immaculate, brush-free finish.

07

Curing & Quality Inspection

Coatings are allowed to cure appropriately before finished components undergo rigorous quality inspection.

08

Reassembly

Doors, drawers, and hardware are carefully reinstalled with precision alignment.

Common Questions

Cabinet Refinishing FAQ

Find expert answers to common questions about our materials, processes, durability, and project timelines.

Can you refinish old wooden cabinets?

Yes! Traditional timber cabinetry is exceptionally well-suited for refinishing. Contractors thoroughly clean, sand, prime, and spray wood cabinets to give them a completely renewed, factory-smooth appearance while preserving your existing woodwork.

Can laminate cabinets be refinished?

Suitable laminate surfaces can be professionally prepared and refinished using specialized high-adhesion primers and bonding coating systems designed to adhere firmly to non-porous surfaces.

Can you change my cabinet colour?

Absolutely. One of the greatest benefits of professional refinishing is the ability to transition from dark dated wood tones to modern crisp whites, contemporary greys, rich navy, sage green, or custom colors.

Is spray painting better than brush painting?

Professional spray application uses specialized equipment to lay down an immaculate, even coat with zero brush marks or roller texture, resulting in a high-end, factory-like finish that standard DIY brushing cannot match.

What finishes are available?

Common sheen levels include matte (understated elegance), satin (balanced and practical), semi-gloss (reflective and easy to clean), and high-gloss (bold and contemporary). Available options vary by contractor and coating system.

Can bathroom vanities be refinished?

Yes. Bathroom vanities and storage cabinetry can be successfully refinished using moisture-resistant coating systems engineered to withstand high humidity and steam exposure.

Do cabinet doors need to be removed?

Yes. Cabinet doors and drawer fronts are carefully removed and labelled as part of a professional process. This allows doors and hardware to be thoroughly sprayed on all edges and returned precisely to their original positions.

How durable is a refinished cabinet?

When executed using professional preparation, high-adhesion primers, and industrial-grade topcoats, refinished cabinets offer exceptional durability that resists everyday wear, cleaning, and cooking residue.

How long does cabinet refinishing take?

Timeframes vary based on the number of doors and drawers, kitchen size, condition, preparation requirements, and curing times. Your contractor can provide a precise estimate after assessing your specific layout.

Is cabinet refinishing cheaper than replacement?

Generally speaking, refinishing your existing cabinetry is significantly less invasive and potentially lower in cost than a full kitchen renovation that involves total demolition and cabinet replacement.
