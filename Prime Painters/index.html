Transparent Pricing Guide

How Much Does Painting Cost in Gauteng?

Painting project pricing varies based on several specific property and surface factors across Johannesburg and surrounding Gauteng areas.

Key Factors That Influence Painting Quotes

✓ Property size & total square meterage
✓ Number of rooms or surface sections
✓ Surface condition (cracks, damp, peeling)
✓ Interior vs. exterior application
✓ Type of paint and coating products specified
✓ Extensive preparation and priming required
✓ Height and architectural accessibility
✓ Labour and specialized equipment requirements

Interior Painting

What affects the price?

Wall condition, number of doors and trim details, ceiling repairs, and premium washable paint selections.

Exterior Painting

What affects the price?

Plaster crack repairs, waterproofing requirements, boundary walls, and high-performance elastomeric coatings.

Roof Painting

What affects the price?

Roof pitch, high-pressure cleaning, tile replacement or sealing, and specialized UV-resistant acrylic membranes.

Commercial Painting

What affects the price?

Project scale, multi-story scaffolding requirements, after-hours scheduling, and heavy industrial coatings.

Troubleshooting & Repairs

Is Your Property Experiencing These Painting Problems?

Environmental exposure, moisture, and normal wear and tear take a toll on Gauteng properties. Our vetted professionals specialize in diagnosing and permanently fixing these common surface issues.

Peeling Paint

Caused by poor initial priming, moisture intrusion, or aging sealants. We scrape, prime, and recoat for lasting adhesion.

Relevant: Exterior & Interior Painting

Cracked Walls

Settlement cracks and hairline fractures in plaster. We apply professional crack-bridging compounds and flexible membranes.

Relevant: Plaster & Wall Restoration

Damp & Moisture Damage

Rising damp and waterproofing failures. Essential treatment requires moisture barriers before any repainting takes place.

Relevant: Waterproofing & Painting

Faded Exterior Paint

Harsh African sun causes UV degradation and color fading. Revitalize curb appeal with high-grade UV-resistant exterior coatings.

Relevant: Exterior Painting

Flaking Roof Coatings

Weathered tile or metal roofs losing protective layers. High-pressure cleaning followed by specialized acrylic roof paint solves this.

Relevant: Roof Painting

Water Stains

Unsightly ceiling and wall discoloration from past leaks. Requires stain-blocking primer and seamless interior repainting.

Relevant: Interior Painting

Damaged Plaster

Chipped, crumbling, or hollow plaster surfaces. Our experts re-plaster and smooth out imperfections prior to painting.

Relevant: Surface Preparation

Dirty or Stained Walls

High-traffic marks, scuffs, and ingrained grime that regular washing cannot fix. A fresh coat brings back a brand-new clean finish.

Relevant: Interior Painting

Outdated Colours

Transform tired, old-fashioned color schemes into modern, stylish palettes that elevate your entire living or working space.

Relevant: Interior & Exterior Painting

Worn Kitchen Cabinets

Scratched, faded, or dated wooden cupboards. Refinishing and spray-painting provide a brand-new kitchen look at a fraction of replacement costs.

Relevant: Cabinet Refinishing
