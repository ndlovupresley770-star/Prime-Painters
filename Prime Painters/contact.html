Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear answers to common questions about our contractor network, quotation process, and services.

1. How quickly will someone respond to my enquiry?

Our customer success team reviews all homeowner requests and contractor partnership queries promptly. We aim to respond within 2 business hours to confirm your submission and start matching your requirements.

2. Do you provide painting quotes?

Yes. Visitors can easily submit their project details through our platform to request accurate quotes from vetted professionals suited to their specific job.

3. Can I request multiple quotes?

Absolutely. Our quote-matching model connects you with multiple suitable local painting professionals so you can conveniently compare pricing, scope, and options.

4. Do you work with residential and commercial customers?

Yes — we cater to both. For residential clients, we handle everything from single rooms to full interior/exterior repaints and kitchen cabinet refinishing. For commercial customers, we manage office buildings, retail spaces, and industrial property coatings with minimal business disruption.

5. What areas do you cover?

We connect homeowners and businesses with painting professionals across Sandton, Midrand, Rosebank, Randburg, and surrounding areas throughout Gauteng.

6. Can I contact PrimePainters about an existing project?

Yes, you can reach out to our support team at any time regarding an active quote or ongoing project coordination.

7. I'm a professional painter. Can I join the network?

Yes! If you are a qualified professional looking for reliable project opportunities, please visit our For Painters section to learn more and submit your application.

8. Do I have to accept a quote?

No, requesting information and receiving quotes does not obligate you to proceed with any contractor. You are free to review your options without pressure.
