Got Questions?

Painting Questions? We've Got Answers.

Everything you need to know about our professional painting network, project preparation, and quotation process.

Q. How much does professional painting cost?

Painting costs vary based on project scale, surface condition, interior versus exterior scope, and specific material selections. We connect you with local professionals who provide tailored, itemized quotes for your exact requirements.

Q. How long does an interior painting project take?

A single room can often be completed in a day or two, while complete multi-room interiors or entire homes may take anywhere from several days to a week, depending on prep work and drying times between coats.

Q. Do painters repair damaged walls before painting?

Yes. Proper surface preparation is essential for a professional finish. Contractors in our network handle minor patching, sanding, crack filling, and priming to ensure a smooth, durable foundation before applying paint.

Q. How many coats of paint are normally required?

Standard practice involves a primer plus two finish coats to achieve rich, true color depth and full opacity. Dramatic color transitions or porous surfaces may require additional coats for ideal coverage.

Q. Should I move furniture before painters arrive?

It is generally recommended to remove small items and valuables. For larger furniture, many contractors are happy to assist with shifting items to the center of the room and covering them securely with protective drop cloths.

Q. Can you paint over existing colors?

Absolutely. With high-quality primers and modern formulations, professionals can effortlessly cover dark colors with lighter shades or transition seamlessly between different palettes.

Q. Do you provide exterior painting services?

Yes, our network includes experienced professionals specializing in exterior siding, stucco, brick, wood, decks, and weather-resistant protective coatings.

Q. Can I request commercial painting quotes?

Yes! We cater to both residential and commercial properties, connecting businesses, offices, retail spaces, and property managers with contractors equipped for larger-scale schedules.

Q. How does the PrimePainters matching process work?

You share your project details and requirements through our simple online request form. We then match you with a vetted, qualified local professional who reaches out to discuss your project and provide a transparent estimate.
